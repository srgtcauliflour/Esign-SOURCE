About SideVault

SideVault is a self-hosted app for browsing community IPA sources and downloading your own App Store apps for personal sideloading, resigning, or archival.

  • Downloads only apps tied to the Apple ID that requests them — you must own or have a free license for the app.
  • Runs best on your own machine or private network. If you expose it to other people over a real network, put it behind HTTPS and set COOKIE_SECURE=true and TRUST_PROXY=true.
  • Each browser session gets its own sandboxed ipatool home directory; credentials are never shared across sessions and are wiped on sign-out or after 24h idle.
  • This tool does not sign or install IPA files itself — pair the downloaded .ipa with your own signer (AltStore, SideStore, Sideloadly, or your own certificate).
